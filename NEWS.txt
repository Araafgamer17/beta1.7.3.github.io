  <h3 style='color: #ffffff; border-bottom: 1px solid #2d3748; padding-bottom: 4px;'>

  <ul style='color: #cbd5e0; line-height: 1.4;'>
    <li><b>1. Magic Fire Spell Refactor (<code style='color: #40a745;'>mg_fire.java</code>):</b>
      <ul style='margin-top: 5px;'>
        <li><b>Fixed Fireball Physics:</b> Overhauled EntityFireball initialization to set velocity vectors directly (<code style='color: #40a745;'>motionX/Y/Z</code>), eliminating momentum loss and trajectory drift in Beta 1.7.3.</li>
        <li><b>Improved Spawn Positioning:</b> Aligned spell launch coordinates directly with the player's eye height and forward vector to prevent self-collision upon casting.</li>
        <li><b>Tier Scaling:</b>
          <ul style='margin-top: 3px;'>
            <li><b>Tier 1:</b> Standard speed casting and block ignition.</li>
            <li><b>Tier 2:</b> Faster fireball velocity with dual Flame/Smoke particle effects.</li>
            <li><b>Tier 3:</b> High-speed projectile launch with dense magical particle bursts.</li>
          </ul>
        </li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    
    <li style='margin-top: 12px;'><b>2. Custom XP & Leveling System (<code style='color: #40a745;'>XPSystem.java</code>):</b>
      <ul style='margin-top: 5px;'>
        <li><b>Player XP System:</b> Integrated a custom experience and leveling mechanic supporting auto-saving (<code style='color: #40a745;'>player_xp.dat</code>) per world folder.</li>
        <li><b>Custom In-Game HUD:</b> Added a green experience progress bar and level display above the hotbar.</li>
        <li><b>Mob Kill XP Integration:</b> Integrated mob experience gains into <code style='color: #40a745;'>EntityLiving.java</code> across passive and hostile mobs.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>
