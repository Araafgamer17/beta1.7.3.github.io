# Arcane Expansion v1.7.3\_01-pre-3

Target Version: Minecraft Beta 1.7.3 Release Stage: Pre-Release Released by: Araafgamer17

This pre-release introduces major magic spell refactoring for Earth and Water, a comprehensive electricity and tech network with hard drives and power grids, a fully overhauled GUI-based enchanting table with categorized enchantment tiers, custom XP systems, and critical NBT compatibility bug fixes for Minecraft Beta 1.7.3.

## New Features & Improvements

### 1. Earth & Water Magic Refactor

- Earth Magic (`mg_earth.java`): Added tiered casting mechanics featuring stone projectile attacks, temporary cobblestone defensive shields, and ground-shaking earthquake bursts.
- Water Magic (`mg_water.java`): Added tiered spells, including ice projectile freezing, water laser beams, and rapid-drowning whirlpool effects.
- Master Wand (`master_wand.java`): Introduced double charge capacity, 50% reduced spell costs, and empowered spell tier boosts.

### 2. Enchanting Overhaul & GUI

- Enchanting Table GUI (`GuiEnchantingTable.java`): Implemented a fully interactive, scrollable GUI with category filters and real-time cost tracking.
- Custom Enchantments: Added support for Protection, Fire Protection, Blast Protection, Projectile Protection, Efficiency, Fortune, Silk Touch, Unbreaking, Mending, Thorns, Sharpness, Smite, Bane of Arthropods, and Speed.
- Enchanted Books: Added reading interfaces (`GuiEnchantedBook.java`, `ItemEnchantedBook.java`) and random Tier 1 book generation.

### 3. Electricity & Tech Network

- Power Grid System: Introduced a power network featuring Energy Cables (`cable.java`), Coal Generators (`coalGenerator.java`), Controllers (`controller.java`), and Batteries (`BlockBattery.java`).
- Hard Disk Drives: Introduced `diskDrive.java` and `harddisk.java`, with capacities ranging from 100 MB to 1 TB (`HDD100MB.java`, `HDD1GB.java`, etc.), alongside a dedicated contents viewer GUI (`GuiDiskContents.java`).
- Components: Added Display blocks (`display.java`) and RAM/CPU items for advanced technology setups.

### 4. Items, Ores & Blood Magic

- Book and Quill: Added `ItemBookAndQuill.java` with fully functional multi-page text editing and an auto-saving GUI (`GuiScreenBookAndQuill.java`).
- Ores: Introduced Copper and Silicon ores through `ModBlocks.java`, with custom chunk generator integration in `ChunkProviderGenerate.java`.
- Blood Magic: Added Sacrificial Daggers (`ItemSacrificialDagger.java`), Blood Vials, Blood Altars (`BlockBloodAltar.java`), and Dark Crystals (`dark_crystal.java`).
- Crystal Polishing: Added mechanics using Sandpaper (`ItemSandPaper.java`) and Raw Crystal Balls to craft polished Crystal Balls.

## Changes & Balance Tweaks

- Magic Crystal Drop Rates: Adjusted drop rates and integration for Magic Crystals across hostile mobs.
- Wand & Equipment Balancing: Overhauled wand durability, charge consumption formulas, and melee combat burst stats.

## Bug Fixes & Technical Improvements

- NBT & ItemStack Serialization: Fixed NBT compound data persistence and deep-copy serialization within `ItemStack.java` using stream-based byte array cloning. Also fixed item stacking, tooltip rendering (`Gui_Inventory.java`), and durability preservation hooks.
- Compatibility & Stability: Fixed Block and Item registration mappings, TileEntity special renderers (`TileEntityPedestalRenderer.java`, `TileEntityCableRenderer.java`), and null-pointer safety checks in `ItemBlock.java`.
- Multiplayer & Singleplayer Fixes: Fixed player death hooks, score handling, and boots NBT synchronization (`for_boots.java`) for Minecraft Beta 1.7.3 multiplayer and singleplayer environments.

## Previous Release: v1.7.3\_01-pre-2
