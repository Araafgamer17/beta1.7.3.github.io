  <!-- NEW FEATURES -->
  <h3 style="color: #ffffff; margin-top: 15px; margin-bottom: 5px;">
    New Features & Improvements
  </h3>
  <ul style="color: #cbd5e0; line-height: 1.4; margin-top: 5px;">
    <li style="margin-top: 8px;"><b>1. Earth & Water Magic Refactor:</b>
      <ul style="margin-top: 5px;">
        <li><b>Earth Magic (<code style="color: #40a745;">mg_earth.java</code>):</b> Added tiered casting mechanics featuring stone projectile attacks, temporary cobblestone defensive shields, and ground-shaking earthquake bursts[cite: 1].</li>
        <li><b>Water Magic (<code style="color: #40a745;">mg_water.java</code>):</b> Added tiered spells including ice projectile freezing, water laser beams, and rapid-drowning whirlpool effects[cite: 1].</li>
        <li><b>Master Wand (<code style="color: #40a745;">master_wand.java</code>):</b> Introduced offering double charge capacity, 50% reduced spell costs, and empowered spell tier boosts[cite: 1].</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    
    <li style="margin-top: 12px;"><b>2. Enchanting Overhaul & GUI:</b>
      <ul style="margin-top: 5px;">
        <li><b>Enchanting Table GUI (<code style="color: #40a745;">GuiEnchantingTable.java</code>):</b> Implemented a fully interactive, scrollable GUI with category filters and real-time cost tracking[cite: 1].</li>
        <li><b>Custom Enchantments:</b> Added support for Protection, Fire/Blast/Projectile Protection, Efficiency, Fortune, Silk Touch, Unbreaking, Mending, Thorns, Sharpness, Smite, Bane of Arthropods, and Speed[cite: 1].</li>
        <li><b>Enchanted Books:</b> Added reading interfaces (<code style="color: #40a745;">GuiEnchantedBook.java</code>, <code style="color: #40a745;">ItemEnchantedBook.java</code>) and random Tier 1 book generation[cite: 1].</li>
      </ul>
    </li>

    <li style="margin-top: 12px;"><b>3. Electricity & Tech Network:</b>
      <ul style="margin-top: 5px;">
        <li><b>Power Grid System:</b> Built featuring Energy Cables (<code style="color: #40a745;">cable.java</code>), Coal Generators (<code style="color: #40a745;">coalGenerator.java</code>), Controllers (<code style="color: #40a745;">controller.java</code>), and Batteries (<code style="color: #40a745;">BlockBattery.java</code>)[cite: 1].</li>
        <li><b>Hard Disk Drives:</b> Introduced <code style="color: #40a745;">diskDrive.java</code> and <code style="color: #40a745;">harddisk.java</code> with capacities ranging from 100 MB to 1 TB (<code style="color: #40a745;">HDD100MB.java</code>, <code style="color: #40a745;">HDD1GB.java</code>, etc.) and a dedicated contents viewer GUI (<code style="color: #40a745;">GuiDiskContents.java</code>)[cite: 1].</li>
        <li><b>Components:</b> Added Display blocks (<code style="color: #40a745;">display.java</code>) and RAM/CPU items for advanced tech setups[cite: 1].</li>
      </ul>
    </li>

    <li style="margin-top: 12px;"><b>4. Items, Ores & Blood Magic:</b>
      <ul style="margin-top: 5px;">
        <li><b>Book and Quill:</b> Added <code style="color: #40a745;">ItemBookAndQuill.java</code> with fully functional multi-page text editing and auto-saving GUI (<code style="color: #40a745;">GuiScreenBookAndQuill.java</code>)[cite: 1].</li>
        <li><b>Ores:</b> Introduced Copper and Silicon ores (<code style="color: #40a745;">ModBlocks.java</code>) with custom chunk generator integration (<code style="color: #40a745;">ChunkProviderGenerate.java</code>)[cite: 1].</li>
        <li><b>Blood Magic:</b> Added Sacrificial Daggers (<code style="color: #40a745;">ItemSacrificialDagger.java</code>), Blood Vials, Blood Altars (<code style="color: #40a745;">BlockBloodAltar.java</code>), and Dark Crystals (<code style="color: #40a745;">dark_crystal.java</code>)[cite: 1].</li>
        <li><b>Crystal Polishing:</b> Added mechanics using Sandpaper (<code style="color: #40a745;">ItemSandPaper.java</code>) and Raw Crystal Balls to craft polished Crystal Balls[cite: 1].</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>
